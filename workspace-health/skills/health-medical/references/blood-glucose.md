# Blood Glucose Reference

## Diagnostic Criteria (ADA 2024)

### Fasting Plasma Glucose
| Category | mmol/L | mg/dL |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Normal | <5.6 | <100 |
| Pre-diabetes (IFG) | 5.6-6.9 | 100-125 |
| Diabetes | >=7.0 | >=126 |

### 2-Hour Post-Meal (OGTT)
| Category | mmol/L | mg/dL |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Normal | <7.8 | <140 |
| Pre-diabetes (IGT) | 7.8-11.0 | 140-199 |
| Diabetes | >=11.1 | >=200 |

### HbA1c
| Category | % | mmol/mol |
|----------|---|----------|
| Normal | <5.7 | <39 |
| Pre-diabetes | 5.7-6.4 | 39-47 |
| Diabetes | >=6.5 | >=48 |

### Estimated Average Glucose (eAG) from HbA1c
| HbA1c (%) | eAG (mmol/L) | eAG (mg/dL) |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| 5.0 | 5.4 | 97 |
| 6.0 | 7.0 | 126 |
| 7.0 | 8.6 | 154 |
| 8.0 | 10.2 | 183 |
| 9.0 | 11.8 | 212 |

## Unit Conversion
- mmol/L x 18 = mg/dL
- mg/dL / 18 = mmol/L

## Self-Monitoring Guidelines

### Monitoring Frequency by Condition
| Condition | Recommended Frequency |
|----------|----------------------|
| Type 1 diabetes | 4-10 times/day (or CGM) |
| Type 2 on insulin | 2-4 times/day |
| Type 2 on oral meds | 1-2 times/day, or per doctor |
| Pre-diabetes | Weekly fasting + post-meal checks |
| General wellness | Periodic fasting checks |

### When to Test
- **Fasting**: First thing in the morning, 8+ hours no food
- **Pre-meal**: Just before eating
- **Post-meal**: 2 hours after first bite of meal
- **Bedtime**: Before sleep
- **Random**: Any time, especially if symptomatic

## CGM (Continuous Glucose Monitor) Interpretation

### Key CGM Metrics
| Metric | Target (non-pregnant adult) |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Time in Range (70-180 mg/dL / 3.9-10.0 mmol/L) | >70% |
| Time Below Range (<70 mg/dL / <3.9 mmol/L) | <4% |
| Time Significantly Below (<54 mg/dL / <3.0 mmol/L) | <1% |
| Time Above Range (>180 mg/dL / >10.0 mmol/L) | <25% |
| Time Significantly Above (>250 mg/dL / >13.9 mmol/L) | <5% |
| Coefficient of Variation (CV) | <36% (stable) |
| GMI (Glucose Management Indicator) | Correlates with HbA1c |

### AGP (Ambulatory Glucose Profile)
- Median line shows typical glucose pattern through the day
- Interquartile range (25th-75th percentile) shows variability
- Wide variability = unstable glucose control
- Narrow band = consistent patterns (good)

GI classification: Low <55, Medium 56-69, High >=70
GL classification: Low <10, Medium 11-19, High >=20

### Strategies to Lower Glycemic Response
1. **Meal sequencing**: Eat vegetables and protein before carbs (reduces spike 30-40%)
2. **Vinegar**: 1-2 tbsp before carb-heavy meal (reduces spike ~20%)
3. **Fiber pairing**: Add fiber to reduce glucose absorption rate
4. **Fat + protein**: Slow gastric emptying when combined with carbs
5. **Post-meal walk**: 15-minute walk reduces peak glucose by 1-2 mmol/L
6. **Cool starches**: Cooled rice/potatoes have more resistant starch (lower GI)

## Hypoglycemia Management

### Severity Levels
| Level | Blood Glucose | Symptoms | Action |
|-------|-------------|----------|--------|
| Level 1 (Alert) | <3.9 mmol/L (<70 mg/dL) | Tremor, sweating, hunger, anxiety | 15-20g fast-acting glucose |
| Level 2 (Serious) | <3.0 mmol/L (<54 mg/dL) | Confusion, difficulty concentrating | 15-20g glucose + seek help |
| Level 3 (Severe) | Any level | Loss of consciousness, seizures | Emergency: glucagon, call 911 |

### Rule of 15
1. Eat 15g of fast-acting carbohydrate (glucose tablets, juice, regular soda)
2. Wait 15 minutes
3. Recheck blood glucose
4. If still <3.9 mmol/L, repeat step 1
5. Once normalized, eat a snack with protein + carbs

### Fast-Acting Glucose Sources (15g)
- 4 glucose tablets
- 120ml fruit juice
- 120ml regular soda (not diet)
- 1 tablespoon honey or sugar
- 6-8 hard candies

## Medication Interactions Affecting Glucose

| Factor | Effect on Glucose |
|--------|------------------|
| Corticosteroids (prednisone) | Significant increase |
| Thiazide diuretics | Mild increase |
| Beta blockers | May mask hypoglycemia symptoms |
| Statins | Slight increase (minimal clinical impact) |
| Metformin | Decrease (primary diabetes drug) |
| GLP-1 agonists | Decrease + weight loss |
| Insulin | Decrease (risk of hypoglycemia) |
| Alcohol | Variable: initial decrease, then increase |

## Risk Factors for Type 2 Diabetes
- Family history (first-degree relative)
- BMI >=25 (>=23 for Asian populations)
- Waist circumference: Male >=90cm, Female >=80cm
- Physical inactivity
- History of gestational diabetes
- Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
- Hypertension (>=140/90)
- HDL <0.90 mmol/L or triglycerides >2.82 mmol/L
- HbA1c 5.7-6.4%, IFG, or IGT
- Age >=45 (screen every 3 years)
