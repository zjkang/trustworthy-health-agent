# Blood Pressure Reference

## Classification (AHA 2017 / ESC 2023)

| Category | Systolic (mmHg) | | Diastolic (mmHg) | Action |
|----------|----------------|---|------------------|--------|
| Normal | <120 | and | <80 | Recheck yearly |
| Elevated | 120-129 | and | <80 | Lifestyle changes, recheck 3-6 months |
| Stage 1 HTN | 130-139 | or | 80-89 | Lifestyle + consider meds if high CVD risk |
| Stage 2 HTN | >=140 | or | >=90 | Lifestyle + medication |
| Hypertensive Crisis | >180 | and/or | >120 | Seek immediate medical care |

Note: Classification is based on the higher category of either systolic or diastolic.

## Measurement Guidelines

### Proper Technique
- Rest 5 minutes before measuring (seated, feet flat, back supported)
- No caffeine, exercise, or smoking 30 minutes before
- Empty bladder
- Use appropriate cuff size (cuff bladder covers 80% of arm circumference)
- Support arm at heart level
- Take 2-3 readings, 1 minute apart; average the last 2

### When to Measure
- **Routine monitoring**: Morning (before medications) and evening
- **New medication**: Daily for first 2 weeks, then twice weekly
- **Stable**: 2-3 times per week minimum
- **Diagnosis confirmation**: At least 2 readings on 2 separate occasions

### Home vs Clinic Readings
- Home readings are typically 5-10 mmHg lower than clinic readings
- Home BP >= 135/85 is equivalent to clinic BP >= 140/90
- ABPM (24h ambulatory) >= 130/80 daytime average = hypertension

## Target Blood Pressure by Population

| Population | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| General adults <65 | <130/80 |
| Adults >=65 | <130/80 (if tolerated) |
| Diabetes | <130/80 |
| CKD | <130/80 |
| Post-stroke | <130/80 |
| Coronary artery disease | <130/80 |

## Lifestyle Modifications (Evidence-Based)

| Intervention | Expected Reduction (systolic) | Evidence Level |
|-------------|-------------------------------|---------------|
| DASH diet | -11 mmHg | Strong |
| Sodium reduction (<1500mg/day) | -5-6 mmHg | Strong |
| Potassium increase (3500-5000mg/day) | -4-5 mmHg | Moderate |
| Weight loss (per 1kg lost) | -1 mmHg | Strong |
| Aerobic exercise (150 min/week) | -5-8 mmHg | Strong |
| Resistance exercise (2-3x/week) | -2-3 mmHg | Moderate |
| Limit alcohol (<=1 drink/day) | -4 mmHg | Moderate |
| Stress management (meditation, deep breathing) | -2-4 mmHg | Limited |

### DASH Diet Key Points
- Rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, low-fat dairy
- Reduce saturated fat, total fat, cholesterol
- Include nuts, legumes, fish
- Limit sweets, sugar-sweetened beverages, red meat
- Sodium <2300mg/day (ideal <1500mg/day)

### High-Potassium Foods
- Banana: 422mg per medium
- Sweet potato: 541mg per medium
- Spinach: 839mg per cup (cooked)
- Avocado: 485mg per half
- White beans: 1004mg per cup
- Salmon: 534mg per 100g

## Medication Classes (Informational Only)

| Class | Examples | Common Side Effects |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| ACE Inhibitors | Enalapril, Lisinopril, Ramipril | Dry cough, hyperkalemia |
| ARBs | Losartan, Valsartan, Irbesartan | Dizziness, hyperkalemia |
| CCBs | Amlodipine, Nifedipine | Ankle edema, flushing |
| Thiazide Diuretics | Hydrochlorothiazide, Indapamide | Hypokalemia, hyperuricemia |
| Beta Blockers | Metoprolol, Bisoprolol | Fatigue, bradycardia |

Note: Medication decisions require physician assessment. This is informational only.

## Risk Factors for Hypertension
- Family history
- Age (risk increases with age)
- Obesity (BMI >=25)
- High sodium diet
- Physical inactivity
- Excessive alcohol consumption
- Chronic stress
- Sleep apnea
- Chronic kidney disease
- Diabetes
